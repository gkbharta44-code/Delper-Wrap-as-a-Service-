1. Executive mandate & vision

Project Delper is a frictionless, sustainable public transit system for 2046. It runs entirely within sovereign domestic corridors, linking India's tier-1 smart cities with elevated overland wraps and stratospheric laser highways.

It is built under a 25-year private BOOT framework. The system turns municipal waste into its own structural graphene, removes the public capital burden, and is designed to run at a net-negative operational cost.

2. Core user experience: Wrap-as-a-Service

The Wrap-as-a-Service (WaaS) journey removes security delays, pilots and check-in queues.

  • App-driven summoning. The passenger books through the local smart city app, and an autonomous 150 kg Aero-Pod is allocated to the nearest urban micro-hangar.
  • The 'Sip & Recline' environment. The cabin holds a stable 1.0 Earth atmosphere pressure. An ultra-high-definition panoramic AR canopy lets the passenger recline and sip a drink in a silent, vibration-free cabin.
  • Frictionless Mach 1 cruise. Linear Synchronous Motors (LSM) move the capsule through a 99.9% pure vacuum at 1,200 km/h, with no cabin ripple or environmental disturbance.

4. The trash-to-graphene resource loop

Flash Joule Heating (FJH) makes raw material directly from unsorted urban garbage, plastics and scrap tyres.

  • The 10-millisecond flash. A high-voltage spike heats municipal waste to 3,000 K. Non-carbon elements flash away as clean gas, leaving premium turbostratic graphene.
  • The cost revolution. Sourcing graphene from urban waste cuts production electricity cost to about $100 per ton, turning a municipal liability into a national infrastructure resource.

6. Seashore manufacturing & spatial footprint

Production is anchored in 100 coastal seashore manufacturing hubs covering 30,000 hectares of state-allocated brownfield or reclaimed maritime land, at a standard 300 hectares per node.

Each factory has automated intake slots, continuous macro-extrusion cores, clean rooms for solar film printing, and deep-water launch dry docks that roll finished segments straight onto the active corridor.

7. Amortization & sovereign handover

  • Capital recovery. Automated high-volume cargo freight and premium luxury transit subscriptions let the consortium recover its full $550 Billion cumulative CapEx within 8.5 years of launch, by 2054.
  • The 2071 transfer. When the 25-year operating block ends in 2071, ownership of the 25,000 km network, the 100 coastal mega-factories, the HAPS fleet and the macro-energy grid passes to the Government of India for a nominal ₹1. The state receives a self-funding, net-profit public utility in near-mint condition.
